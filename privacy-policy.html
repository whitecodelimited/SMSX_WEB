Privacy Policy

General Information

  • This policy explains what data may be processed while you use the SMSX application and the purposes for which that data may be used.
  • The application processes limited data to manage virtual number activation, credit transactions, order history, and technical security operations.

Data We May Collect

  • When an order is created, usage data such as the service name, country, number, SMS code status, activation time, and transaction price may be stored.
  • For purchases and credit transactions, product identifiers, transaction date, credited amount, device identifiers, and store transaction details may be processed.
  • To keep the application secure and operational, technical logs related to device identity, app usage, and errors may also be processed.

How We Use Data

  • Data is used to provide numbers, display SMS status, update credit balances, verify purchase records, and prevent abuse.
  • Data may also be used on a limited basis to review support requests, perform security checks, and improve service quality.

Retention and Sharing

  • Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service. Order history, credit records, and purchase records may be kept to preserve account integrity.
  • Personal data is not sold to third parties without authorization unless required by law. Limited sharing may occur with service providers involved in payment, infrastructure, analytics, or purchase verification processes.

Your Rights

  • Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your data, request corrections, or request deletion.
  • Requests submitted through the application or support channels will be reviewed within a reasonable time.
